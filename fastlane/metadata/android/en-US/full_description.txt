Make pizza dough with confidence, whether this is your first batch or your hundredth.

Pizza Maker gives you two ways to calculate:

• Guided walks you through one clear choice at a time and applies sensible defaults.
• Expert keeps every important parameter in one precise, compact workspace.

Your calculation stays with you as a single Draft, even when you switch between Guided and Expert. When a dough is worth keeping, save it to My doughs: open it as a document, review its complete Method, duplicate or rename it, and adjust a copy without changing the original.

Every calculation produces exact ingredient weights and a timed Method through to baking. Contextual info sheets explain hydration, fermentation, flour strength, yeast and poolish right where you need them.

Looking for inspiration? Browse the built-in Recipe collection, discover the toppings for Margherita, Marinara and Reine, then prepare the suggested dough in one tap.

Pizza Maker works offline and includes:

• Guided and Expert dough calculators
• Direct and poolish doughs
• User defaults for your usual hydration, salt and dough-ball weight
• A reusable My doughs library
• Timed, step-by-step Methods
• Curated topped-pizza Recipes
• English and French
• System, light and dark appearances
• Keep-awake support while you cook

No account. No connection required. Just a dependable tool for better pizza.
